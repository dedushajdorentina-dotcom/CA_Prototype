Get your book ready

Read Goodnight, Tinku! out loud, just like always. Your buddy listens and joins in a few times.

1. Hold your own book.
Paper copy or any copy you have at home.
2. Set the phone nearby.
Face up, close enough to hear you both. Leave the screen on.
3. Just read.
Your buddy joins in after a quiet pause. Wait for your child's answer.
Private by design.
The microphone is only on during the story. Sound is checked live and thrown away. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
